About the System

The Campus Maintenance Reporting System helps students report maintenance problems such as damaged fans, non-working lights, leaking taps, damaged furniture, washroom issues, and Wi-Fi problems directly to campus maintenance staff.

What Can You Report?

🌀

Fan Problems

Report damaged, noisy, or non-working ceiling fans.

💡

Light Problems

Report broken tubes, dark classrooms, or flickering lights.

🚰

Water & Plumbing

Report leaking taps, low water pressure, and washroom issues.

🪑

Furniture & Infrastructure

Report damaged desks, broken chairs, doors, and boards.
